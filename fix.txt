Here are some powerful features we could add to the banner system to make it a more robust marketing tool for your food business:

**1. Time-Based Scheduling (Start & Expiry Dates)**
Instead of manually toggling banners on and off, you could set an automatic start and end date/time. 
* *Use Case:* You want to run a "Weekend Special" or "Happy Hour" promotion that automatically goes live on Friday evening and disappears on Sunday night without you having to touch the dashboard.

**2. Audience Targeting (Segmentation)**
Show specific banners only to specific types of customers rather than everyone.
* *Use Case:* Show a "Welcome! Get 10% off your first order" banner **only** to new users. Or, show a "We Miss You!" discount banner **only** to customers who haven't placed an order in the last 30 days.

**3. Analytics & Click Tracking (CTR)**
Track the performance of your marketing campaigns. The system would record how many times a banner was viewed (impressions) and how many times it was clicked.
* *Use Case:* You run two different banner designs for a new pizza and can check the dashboard to see which one generated more clicks.

**4. Different Banner Types (Formats)**
Right now, banners are just images in a carousel. We could add different formats:
* **Announcement/Top Bar:** A thin, text-only dismissible bar at the very top of the screen (e.g., "Store is closing in 30 minutes!").
* **Popup Modals:** A banner that pops up on the screen the very first time a user opens the app that day.
* **Video/GIF Support:** Allowing looping videos instead of just static images to make the food look more appetizing.

**5. Automated Coupon Linking**
Instead of just linking to a URL, you could link a banner directly to a Coupon code in your database. 
* *Use Case:* A customer clicks the banner, and it automatically applies the promo code to their cart so they don't have to type it out at checkout.
